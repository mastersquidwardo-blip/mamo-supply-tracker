How we get from “seen” to “sold” to “print”

  1. Proven sold = first-party mass we measured + TCGPlayer sealed + Amazon marketplace “bought” badges.
  2. Estimated sold = stretch mass a bit for missed doors, stretch TCGPlayer for the rest of hobby, keep marketplace almost as-is (only a 3–5% overlap cushion).
  3. Estimated print = estimated sold + about 15% still unsold in stores / warehouses (first month).

We do not treat Amazon marketplace as automatic double-counting with TCGPlayer. At ~$35 retail / ~$42 market, flipping usually loses money after fees. The small cushion is for rare anomalies only.

    Grand Master Rare check

    This site estimates boxes sold and printed. It is not yet a census of serial pulls.

    Konami locked Americas Grand Master Rares at 1,800 (18 names × 100). If estimated print (~220k) is right, that is about 1 GMR per 122 tuck boxes — not the “1 in 881” story (that needs ~1.6M boxes of print).

    A few five-figure eBay hits plus lots of dead air in filmed openings is what 1-in-122 looks like when only a sliver of opens get posted. Public listings do not by themselves blow up 220k.

    Still missing: a counted opening ledger (boxes opened vs GMRs found). Until that exists, the GMR cell stays empty — we will not invent it or average it into the box math.
