# GMR serial bridge (confirmed → boxes opened)

Uses the public serial tracker:

https://mastersquidwardo-blip.github.io/mamo/

## Idea

1. Count **confirmed Americas** serial identities (not Europe `E` serials).
2. Guess what share of pulled Americas GMRs become public (default **25%**, band **15–40%**).
3. Implied pulled ≈ confirmed ÷ that share.
4. At the current print estimate, boxes per GMR ≈ print ÷ 1,800 (~122 at 220k).
5. Implied **boxes opened** ≈ implied pulled × boxes per GMR.

That bands **opens**, not sealed sold. Many sold boxes are never opened in week 1.

## Tolerance

| Public report share | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 40% | Lots of pulls get posted → fewer implied opens |
| 25% | Mid |
| 15% | Most pulls stay private → more implied opens |

If a future counted opening ledger (boxes opened + hits) disagrees with sell-through by more than 2×, say which side is weak — do not average blindly.
